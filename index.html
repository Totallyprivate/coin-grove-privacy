Coin Grove - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Coin Grove (the "app") is an offline expense tracker. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What you enter stays on your device

Everything you enter - expenses, categories, comments, dates, budgets, savings goals, debts, financial goals, exchange rates and your settings (language, theme, currency, look) - is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No internet permission: the app cannot connect to the internet. It never downloads exchange rates; the rates are estimates stored on your device, and you can edit them.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or SMS.

Android backup

If automatic backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your device and Google account settings. The developer cannot see or access it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then Coin Grove, then Storage, and choose Clear data. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Coin Grove is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not financial advice

Coin Grove is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not connect to banks, cards or payment services. Exchange rates inside the app are approximate.
